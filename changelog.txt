WaEnhancer 1.6.7

[UPDATED]
* Merged upstream through 0352170c. Supports WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business 2.26.38.xx through 2.26.45.xx.

[NEW]
* Enable WhatsApp's native floating navigation bar and customize its colors.
* Restore local stickers to Favorites from the manager on rooted devices. Review the account and sticker count before restoring.
* Download pending statuses directly. A notification shows hook loading progress when WhatsApp rebuilds its cache.

[IMPROVED]
* Custom Privacy shows the global setting beside each per-contact override. Locked contacts can also be hidden from call history, contact pickers and statuses.
* Disabling Meta AI turns off more entry points. The sticker outline setting now also covers sticker styles and video frames.
* Faster hook startup and lower CPU use. Updated Italian translations and compact manager progress dialogs.

[FIXED]
* Fixed contact verification, contact names and quoted message links on newer WhatsApp versions.
* Fixed wallpaper transparency leaking into other screens and unreadable selected chips with custom colors.
* Root diagnostics no longer block the main thread. Custom keyboxes accept PKCS#8 private keys.

[COMPATIBILITY]
* Requires Android 13 or later and an Xposed framework supporting libxposed API 101.
